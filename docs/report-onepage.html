Nothing is deleted. Every entry keeps working. Short ones simply stop matching inside longer words.

No list review. No new data. It also covers words nobody has listed yet — surnames, place names, next year's slang.

Server-side only. Ship it behind a config value, set it to 4, revert by setting it back.

This is not “filter less”

The same design also lets abuse through: spaced-out text, full-width characters and look-alike letters all pass untouched, and only 6 of 25 common English swear words are on the list at all.

The filter is simultaneously too harsh and too weak, for one reason. Fixing it makes it more accurate in both directions.

Why it happens

The list is 95,660 entries, 93% Chinese — sound for the market it was written for. Rebirth launched in China first, and the list travelled with the build.

30,262 of those entries are 1–3 characters. As fragments they sit inside ordinary words: te in ignite, ur in curse.
